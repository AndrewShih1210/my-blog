Review an example before planning a workshop

  1. Choose one real task, such as lesson design, document preparation or message handling, rather than only naming tools.
  2. Review the linked materials or recordings below and decide whether your team needs an introduction, demonstration or hands-on workshop.
  3. Email the audience, dates, attendance, equipment and intended output so scope and format can be agreed. This English page provides information; the delivery language must be confirmed separately.

Practical questions

What should an invitation include?

Please include your organization, audience, approximate attendance, work or teaching needs, preferred dates, venue or online format, and device or account constraints.

How are scope, duration and fees agreed?

Scope, format, duration, fees and availability are discussed by email. This page does not promise a fixed price, session length or booking slot.

What equipment and materials are needed?

Device, network, software and account needs depend on the topic. Start with public or de-identified examples; do not email confidential documents or identifiable raw data.

May we record or publish materials?

Please agree on recording, material sharing and publication before the event. Existing public videos do not grant equivalent permissions for a new engagement.
