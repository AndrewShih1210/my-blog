This repository presents Yu-Ting Shih's research outputs in a GitHub-friendly format, turning source PDFs into readable article pages and extracted visual assets.

Chinese version: [README.md](./README.md)

## Biography

Yu-Ting Shih is a third-year Ph.D. student in the Graduate Institute of Educational Information and Measurement at National Taichung University of Education and was approved as a doctoral candidate on December 15, 2025.

He currently serves as a part-time instructor at National Taichung University of Education and is actively involved in AI teaching, research projects, and higher-education AI literacy initiatives.

His research and practice focus on generative AI, educational technology, learning analytics, conversational agents, and the automation of teaching and assessment workflows.

He holds an invention patent as well as Google Education and Gemini-related certifications, and continues to participate in academic exchanges and professional development related to AI, data science, research ethics, and digital learning.

The biography above is adapted from `施育廷_2026.docx.pdf`.
