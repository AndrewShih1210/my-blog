Clarify the task first

Separate a teaching or work task into data, processing steps and interaction before deciding whether AI is needed.

Practice and debug together

Use plain language to support beginners while retaining hands-on operation, testing and revision.

Keep people responsible for review

Treat AI output as a draft to check. People remain responsible for sources, academic ethics and usage limits.

Choose a learning path

Move from concepts to guided practice. Start with a relevant task, not an exhaustive tool list.
