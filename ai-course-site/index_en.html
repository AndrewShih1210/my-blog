Learning Path

Core course arc

The course follows a clear sequence: conceptual grounding, prompt design, tool choice, teaching application, research application, workflow integration, interactive services, and governance.

01

Understand what AI is

Build a stable mental model for generative AI, multimodal systems, and agent-based applications.

02

Learn to ask better

Turn vague requests into structured prompts and reusable interaction patterns.

03

Choose tools intentionally

Select platforms according to task type, output needs, and collaboration context.

04

Apply AI to real work

Translate tools into concrete classroom, research, and administrative workflows.

Units

Eight instructional units

Each unit includes learning goals, activity ideas, reflection tasks, and implementation suggestions.

Unit 1

Generative AI and new digital work patterns

Understand how AI changes the way people work, learn, and make decisions.

 Unit 2

Prompt design and dialogue strategies

Build reusable prompt structures and improve output control.

 Unit 3

Choosing AI tools and platforms

Compare chat, research, productivity, and workflow-oriented tools.

 Unit 4

AI for instructional design

Use AI to support lesson planning, assessment, feedback, and classroom interaction.

 Unit 5

AI for academic research

Explore literature review, idea development, writing support, and research workflows.

 Unit 6

AI workflows and cloud integration

Turn documents, forms, spreadsheets, and AI tools into repeatable systems.

 Unit 7

AI agents, chatbots, and interactive services

Move from single prompts to service-oriented AI experiences.

 Unit 8

Ethics, risk, and quality control

Build governance habits for accuracy, privacy, bias, and publishing boundaries.
