Unit 05

AI for academic research

Treat AI as a support system for research work, not as a substitute for scholarly judgment.

In this unit

GoalsKey ideasVisual casesActivitiesTaskNext unit

Learning goals

  • Use AI to support reading, organizing, and framing research work.
  • Recognize citation, fabrication, and bias risks in AI-supported research.
  • Build a transparent research workflow.

Key ideas

In research settings, AI often helps most with organization and comparison rather than interpretation and methodological judgment.

Learners should therefore document both what AI did and how they checked or revised its work.

Visual cases

This cover situates AI inside a research-support context rather than as a substitute for scholarly judgment.

The interface visual supports discussion of source-aware reading, synthesis, and cross-checking.

Activities

Activity 1: Cross-checking summaries

Compare a human summary with an AI summary of the same reading and identify missing or distorted points.

Activity 2: Narrowing a research topic

Use AI to brainstorm, then refine the result into a specific and researchable question.

Task

Submit a short research workflow note explaining how you would use AI for reading, synthesis, and writing support.

Further viewing

Use the channel's research-oriented videos, literature organization walkthroughs, and platform demonstrations to reinforce source checking, summary review, and the boundary of scholarly responsibility.
