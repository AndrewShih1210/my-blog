Unit 06

AI workflows and cloud integration

The most valuable AI use is often not a single conversation, but a repeatable process.

In this unit

GoalsKey ideasActivitiesTaskNext unit

Learning goals

  • Understand the logic of connecting forms, documents, spreadsheets, and AI tools.
  • Describe repeated work in terms of inputs, processing, outputs, and review points.
  • Design one workflow blueprint.

Key ideas

Workflow design becomes more stable when learners clearly define inputs, processing steps, outputs, and human checkpoints.

This is why workflow thinking appears after conceptual understanding and tool choice in the course sequence.

Activities

Activity 1: Work decomposition

Select one repeated task and break it into data input, rules, output format, and exception handling.

Activity 2: Workflow blueprint

Draw a diagram or structured list that explains an AI-enabled process and its review points.

Task

Submit a workflow blueprint describing purpose, data sources, steps, outputs, and risk controls.
